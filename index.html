How to request account deletion

You can request deletion of your Pinoy Prime Booking Services account and associated personal data even if you can no longer sign in to the app.

  1. Send an email to infopinoyprime@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address registered to your account and indicate whether you use the app as a Customer or Worker. Do not send your password.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

Data that will be deleted

After we verify your request, we will delete your Supabase authentication account, personal profile information, and associated personal data that is no longer required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Data that may be retained

Some booking, transaction, or security records may be retained when necessary to comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or enforce our agreements. Retained information will be limited to what is necessary and will be protected from unauthorized access.

Retention period — confirmation required before publication.

The exact retention period for booking, transaction, and security records must be stated here after the developer confirms the applicable retention schedule. Do not publish this page or submit its URL to Google Play until this section is completed with the actual period and the deletion process has been verified.

Request processing

We will review verified deletion requests and contact you if additional information is needed. The account and data deletion process must be completed in accordance with our actual operational procedures and applicable legal requirements.
